# Design DNA

## Character
- cinematic forest / graphite environment;
- warm sand accent used sparingly;
- condensed editorial headings;
- documentary field tone rather than SaaS/product UI;
- large photography integrated into composition;
- generous negative space;
- hard edges and thin borders instead of oversized rounded cards.

## Landing V1 is the visual root
The original single-page landing remains the source of visual identity: immersive dark scenes, large real photography, soft transitions, restrained sand accents and clear recruitment narrative.

The production home page should preserve the logical V1 sequence where it still serves recruitment: hero → team → what we do → how to join → team life/events → who we look for → first contact. New portal functionality must not replace those strong blocks with generic editorial substitutes.

## Page-specific grammar
Shared visual DNA does **not** mean shared page templates. Every major public page needs a composition derived from its user task:

- **Home** — recruitment landing narrative and immersive scenes.
- **Team** — documentary dossier / sequence of group states, not another landing.
- **Start** — chronological first-visit route with practical checkpoints.
- **Gear** — sticky interactive loadout laboratory centered on the fighter and equipment layers.
- **Events** — seasonal timeline/list organized by dates and event formats.
- **Application** — focused split-screen conversion task with no extra promotional sections.

Do not solve page consistency by repeating the same hero + 3 image cards + manifesto + full-bleed CTA on every URL.

## Image language
Окружение: лесные полигоны, земля, трава, сосны, естественный свет. Экипировка в приглушённой природной палитре. В кадре важна рабочая ситуация, а не позирование.

## Composition library
Cinematic landing scene, documentary dossier, chronological route, sticky interactive scene, seasonal timeline, split conversion screen, full-bleed photography, restrained text rows and image sequences.

A composition is selected by context; it is not a mandatory section set.

## Motion
Только reveal, subtle image motion and interaction feedback. Всегда учитывать `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Anti-patterns
Glassmorphism, glow, acid gradients, generic SaaS cards, одинаковые 3-column grids на каждой странице, одинаковый hero на всех разделах, repeating chapter/manfiesto patterns regardless of context, pill badges before every heading, fake stats/counters, decorative dashboard UI.
